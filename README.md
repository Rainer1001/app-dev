# My Favorite Series: Avatar: The Last Airbender
 
> A story about **balance**, friendship, and growing up.
 
---
 
## About the Series
 
*Avatar: The Last Airbender* is an animated fantasy series that aired from 2005 to 2008.
It follows a young boy named Aang, who must learn to master all four elements
and help end a long war.
 
## Quick Facts
 
| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Creators | Michael Dante DiMartino and Bryan Konietzko |
| Years aired | 2005 to 2008 |
| Seasons (Books) | 3: Water, Earth, Fire |
| Episodes | 61 |
 
## Main Characters
 
1. **Aang** - the last Airbender and the Avatar
2. **Katara** - a Water Tribe waterbender and healer
3. **Sokka** - a Water Tribe warrior and strategist
4. **Toph** - an Earthbender and Aang's teacher
5. **Zuko** - a Fire Nation prince on a journey of change
 
## Why I Like It
 
- Strong character development
- Great story and world-building
  - Four nations, four elements
  - Lessons about friendship and responsibility
- Memorable humor and music
